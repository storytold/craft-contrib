# RFC-0000: How we make decisions

Updated by: [RFC-0013](0013-amending-rfcs.md)

Changes to this repo, and decisions that affect several apps, are made
through RFCs: pull requests here that add `rfcs/NNNN-short-title.md`.
This process is changed the same way.

- Maintainers are the `craft-app-team` GitHub team. Owners are the
  storytold org's admins, and are also maintainers.
- Anyone can open an RFC or comment on one.
- A maintainer starts the comment period by commenting its end date:
  7 days for adding or removing an app or changing this process,
  3 days for everything else.
- After the period, the RFC merges with approval from a maintainer other
  than the author, if no maintainer's objection is unresolved. An objection
  gives a reason and says what would resolve it.
- An owner can decide an objection, or merge or close an RFC early, and
  says why on the PR.
